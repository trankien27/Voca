# Tên bộ từ: Tuần 8 — Tổng ôn + IELTS vocabulary
Cấp độ: B2–C1
Mô tả: Từ nối, mô tả số liệu, từ và collocation học thuật cho IELTS.

## Ngày 1 — Từ nối và diễn đạt quan điểm

| STT | Từ | Phiên âm | Loại | Nghĩa | Ví dụ |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | however | /haʊˈevə(r)/ | adv | tuy nhiên | The idea sounds simple; however, it is difficult to put into practice. |
| 2 | furthermore | /ˌfɜːðəˈmɔː(r)/ | adv | hơn nữa | Furthermore, the plan is too expensive. |
| 3 | although | /ɔːlˈðəʊ/ | conj | mặc dù | Although the proposal has some merits, it raises several concerns. |
| 4 | for instance | /fər ˈɪnstəns/ | phr | ví dụ như | Many cities, for instance, have introduced stricter rules on noise. |

| 6 | therefore | /ˈðeəfɔː(r)/ | adv | do đó, vì vậy | The data is incomplete; therefore, no firm conclusion can be reached. |
| 7 | on the other hand | /ɒn ði ˈʌðə hænd/ | phr | mặt khác | On the other hand, living alone can give young people more independence. |
| 8 | in addition | /ɪn əˈdɪʃn/ | phr | ngoài ra | In addition, the scheme would create opportunities for local businesses. |
| 9 | whereas | /ˌweərˈæz/ | conj | trong khi (đối lập) | Some people prefer city life, whereas others value the peace of the countryside. |
| 10 | consequently | /ˈkɒnsɪkwəntli/ | adv | kết quả là, vì thế | Prices rose sharply; consequently, demand began to fall. |
| 11 | moreover | /mɔːrˈəʊvə(r)/ | adv | hơn thế nữa | Moreover, the policy has received strong public support. |
| 12 | with regard to | /wɪð rɪˈɡɑːd tə/ | phr | về vấn đề, liên quan đến | With regard to cost, the second option is clearly more attractive. |

| 14 | to some extent | /tə sʌm ɪkˈstent/ | phr | ở một mức độ nào đó | I agree with this view to some extent, but not entirely. |

| 17 | conversely | /ˈkɒnvɜːsli/ | adv | ngược lại | Conversely, a decrease in supply tends to push prices up. |
| 18 | hence | /hens/ | adv | vì thế, do đó | The method is quick and cheap, hence its popularity. |
| 19 | albeit | /ˌɔːlˈbiːɪt/ | conj | mặc dù, dẫu cho | The situation has improved, albeit slowly. |
| 20 | notwithstanding | /ˌnɒtwɪθˈstændɪŋ/ | prep | bất chấp, mặc dù | Notwithstanding these difficulties, the project was a success. |
